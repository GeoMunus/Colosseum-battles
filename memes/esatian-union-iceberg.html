The Esatian Union Iceberg

18 nations · one Gerig · 700 years of lore

Above the surface

What every citizen knows

  • The Esatian Union: 18 member nations, 50,000,000+ people
  • Founded in 1776 by a poverty rebellion inside the Barthic Empire
  • The Union is headed by a single representative called the Gerig
  • Current Gerig Lorvan Madin is a hero of the Tadar War and the liberation of Atharas & Ijalia

Just below the waterline

Civics-class trivia

  • Three branches with alien names: Leganic, Sotonic, and Modelic
  • All three Union-wide parties (UNP, UGP, UWP) run on the exact same plank: "strong emphasis on national unity"
  • Nuswarm is just the British colony "New Warmth" that Lyance bought in 1902
  • Two rival faiths: Coraminga (hard-line trinitarian) vs. Rebinga (God is humanity)

Sunlight zone

Stuff the history nerds bring up

  • Naven is openly an oligarchy — inside a Union whose constitution bans "unjust concentration of power"
  • Gerig Dexter Robins gave the entire State of Lyance to his wife for a month… as a birthday gift (1915)
  • He then tried to pass a bill making his self-invented national dance, "The Wiggle," mandatory
  • Gerig Bargal Hopel put a Union-wide cap on the minimum wage (1987)

Twilight zone

The parts the textbooks soften

  • 1967: Quebec's military killed hundreds of women's-rights protestors, many of them ethnic Esatians — and Esatia surrendered
  • The Gerig has been constitutionally banned from declaring war since 1975 — yet Esatia keeps ending up in (and winning) wars
  • Gerig Sutlam "The Target" Arkas had a 200,000ǥ bounty on his head — and after the hit, the hitman and his boss were actually paid, half and half
  • The Barthic Empire's war on Britain drove King Thomas of Britain to suicide (1745)

Midnight zone

Whispered in Nuswarm City

  • Gerig Khabora Dagilidan, the beloved hunter-craftsman who almost sparked a technological revolution… killed and ate his wife (1793)
  • Mila Lenking, "Esatia's Only Queen" and feminist icon, died in 2009 of an "unexpected blow to the temple" — no further explanation recorded
  • 1646: a lone Stevic man massacred a crowd in Abburan and nearly ignited a civil war
  • July 2024: party leader Hanas Sagil incited the riot that killed 15+ Esatians outside the Barthic capital — he is still a party leader

The trench

Sources the archive won't shelve

  • The 1712 joint British–Barthic colony in India ran on transported African slave labor — the Union's founding wealth was "negotiated resources"
  • The Calistan Rebels' slave revolt (1738) is what actually cracked the Barthic Empire — the "poverty rebellion" came after
  • Dyskonture was admitted to the Union in 2022 and expelled within a year for state violence against Ontanans — the vetting program never flagged it

The future is already written

Below all light

  • 2038: Gerig Kipa Kilophi's Imperial Party seizes the government and suppresses all dissent
  • Kipa is assassinated by the anti-treason company he himself founded
  • 2066: the civil war ends with the Union carved evenly between Ontana, Dyskonture, and Quebec
  • Andon Balexti and the Balextitek Kingsmen rule the ruins until 2095
  • The Esatian Union has about forty years left. No one inside it knows.
