# "I Know How This Works" — Weird Al-Style Parody

**Target:** "I Know How This Works" (original Suno track, 2025)
**Premise:** The original is a confident call-out of a con artist. The parody keeps
the exact same swagger — but the narrator is a guy assembling flat-pack furniture
who refuses, on principle, to read the manual. He does not know how this works.
The title stays verbatim (the "Whatever You Like" maneuver); the dramatic irony
does all the work.

**Weird Al techniques used:** unreliable first-person narrator with total sincerity,
mundane domestic subject swap, verse-2 escalation (visible disaster, undimmed
confidence), hyper-specific detail (Allen wrench, cam-locks, twelve leftover screws),
line-for-line syllable and rhyme matching.

with injuries), the bridge is the classic spoken-word weirdness, and the final
chorus swaps the stab lines for the sounds his furniture now makes.
